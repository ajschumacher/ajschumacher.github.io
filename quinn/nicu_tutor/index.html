Machines of the NICU

The NICU (say it: “NIK-yoo”) is the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit — the part of the hospital where the newest, smallest, sickest babies get round-the-clock care. It is full of amazing machines, and every machine has a story about how the body works.

These lessons are for future NICU doctors. Each one takes about 20 minutes. Read carefully, look at the pictures, then take the quiz at the end. Score 6 out of 8 or better and you earn that machine's badge. Earn all nine and you've completed your first NICU rotation. 🎓

💬 A note before you start

The NICU is a hopeful place, but it is also a serious one. Some babies in the NICU are fighting for their lives, and not all of them make it. These lessons tell you the truth about that, because real doctors need real information — and because the story of the NICU is mostly a story of how doctors, nurses, scientists, and families keep making things better, year after year.
