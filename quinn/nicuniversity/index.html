Welcome, first-year pre-med student. This is your timetable. Each week runs Monday 9:00 onward — lectures, recitations where you do the homework, and a final exam on Friday afternoon. Following it in order is the smart way through, but every class is open whenever you want it.

🏆 Course badges:
🗺️ How the week works
  • Lectures (about 20 minutes) teach something new and end with an 8-question quiz. 6 of 8 earns that course's badge.
  • Recitations are where you do the printed homework assigned at the end of each lecture (about 20 minutes). Your professor grades it with the answer key and records the score.
  • Friday: study hall, then that week's Final Exam covering everything. Every fifth week the final is a bigger Unit Exam.
  • Every class has a 🔊 read-aloud button in the margin of each paragraph, and dotted words you can tap for a definition.

Every built week is open in any order. Your NICU Tutor badges live over at NICU Tutor.
