Homework 1: The Instruction Book

A decoding puzzle, some chromosome arithmetic, twin sorting, and your own family tree — about 20 minutes on paper.

How recitation works

  1. Print the homework below. Colored pencils are nice for the family tree but not required.
  2. Do the work on paper. For the decoding puzzle, put a finger on the code table and read the message three letters at a time — cells do exactly this. Stuck? The Genetics lecture has the diagram.
  3. Your professor grades it with the answer key.
  4. Record the score and earn the Genetics homework stamp 🔡.

🧪 Bonus while you work

Look at the people in your family tree. Pick one feature — eye color, curly or straight hair, whether you can roll your tongue — and see who shares it with whom. Every match is a recipe passed from a volume of someone's book into yours. (Some features come from several genes at once, so the pattern won't always be neat. Real genetics rarely is.)
