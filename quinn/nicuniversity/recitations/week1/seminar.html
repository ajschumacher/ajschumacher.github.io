Recitation · Freshman Seminar · Monday 2:30

Homework 1: Notes Practice

Recitation is a working session — no lecture, just you, a pencil, and about 20 minutes. Today you'll practice the one rule of note-taking on a real paragraph, and look back at yesterday's Class Notes sheets.

How recitation works

  1. Print the homework (link below). Grab your Class Notes sheets from Monday's three classes, too — you'll want them for Part C.
  2. Do the work on paper, in your own words. If you get stuck, it's fine to open the Freshman Seminar lecture and look — that's what notes and lectures are for.
  3. Your professor grades it with the answer key. Different words that mean the same thing get full credit.
  4. The score goes on your transcript and you earn the Freshman Seminar homework stamp 📓.

💡 Recitation tip

Part B asks you to turn a paragraph about the pulse oximeter into three big ideas. Try this: read the paragraph once without writing anything. Then cover it and say out loud what it was about. That — what you remember without looking — is usually your first big idea.
