Homework 1: Growth Rates

Subtract, divide, plot, and read the line — about 20 minutes on paper. This is real calculus thinking done with numbers you can handle, which is exactly how mathematicians started too.

How recitation works

  1. Print the homework below. A pencil with an eraser is best for the plotting part.
  2. Do the work on paper. Write the subtraction and the division for every rate — the steps earn points. For plotting, find the day along the bottom first, then go up to the weight. Stuck? The Calculus lecture has a worked example.
  3. Your professor grades it with the answer key.
  4. Record the score and earn the Calculus homework stamp ✏️.

💡 Recitation tip

When a rate comes out as a “minus” number, don't panic — it just means the line goes downhill. Say it out loud: “minus fifty grams per day means losing fifty grams a day.” Negative numbers are much friendlier when they describe something you can picture.
