💡 Recitation tip

A quick way to check a mean: it must land between the smallest and biggest numbers. If your five heart rates run from 130 to 160 and your mean comes out 725, you forgot to divide. If it comes out 45, you divided something wrong. The check takes two seconds and catches most mistakes — real statisticians do it constantly.
