🧪 Bonus experiment

Find a balloon. Blow it up a little and squeeze it gently between your palms: you're raising the pressure inside, and you can feel it push back everywhere at once. Now press it with one fingertip instead — same push, tiny area, and the balloon dents deeply right there. Pressure = push ÷ area, in your hands.
