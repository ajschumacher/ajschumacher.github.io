How recitation works

  1. Print the homework below and keep your Seminar Class Notes sheet next to you.
  2. Do the work on paper. For Part A, read the passage twice, then cover it and write the idea from memory (that's retrieval practice). For Part C, write the right answer first, then ask: what would a kid who half-understood say? Stuck? The Seminar lecture is one click away.
  3. Your professor grades it with the answer key (partial credit is built in).
  4. Record the score and earn this week's Seminar homework stamp 📓.
