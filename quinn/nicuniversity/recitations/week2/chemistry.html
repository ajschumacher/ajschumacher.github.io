🔬 Home lab: the red-cabbage pH tester

The last page of the homework is a real experiment: red-cabbage juice is a natural indicator that turns pink in acids and blue-green in bases. Test the kitchen, line the cups up from most acid to most base, and you've built Sørensen's ruler. It isn't graded — the professor just initials it when it's done. Grown-up needed for the hot water.
