✅ The big ideas, by course

🎓 Freshman Seminar — reread
  • A few sentences carry a lecture; the rest support them. Three tests: “so what?”, “could I forget it?”, “is it repeated?”
  • Ideas hide in headings, first and last sentences, and the lesson of a story. Tables, dates, and most numbers are details.
  • Testing yourself beats rereading (about 60% vs 40%). Close the page and say it, teach it, or write a quiz question.
⚗️ Chemistry 101 — reread
  • Acids let go of loose hydrogen and taste sour; bases grab it and feel slippery. Mixed, they cancel (neutralize).
  • pH is the acid ruler: 0–14, 7 neutral, lower = more acid. Each step is ten times.
  • Blood must stay at 7.35–7.45. CO₂ makes carbonic acid; bicarbonate is the sponge; lungs (fast) and kidneys (slow) keep the balance. A blood gas reads pH, CO₂, and bicarbonate as clues.
🔬 Biology 101 — reread
  • The heart is two pumps with four rooms: right side to the lungs, left side to the body. Arteries out, veins back, valves one-way.
  • Blood = plasma carrying red cells (oxygen), white cells (defense), platelets (plugs). About 80 mL per kg — a 1 kg preemie has a third of a cup.
  • Before birth, blood skips the lungs through the ductus arteriosus; it should close at birth. In preemies it often stays open (PDA), flooding the lungs.
🧲 Physics 101 — reread
  • White light is all the colors mixed; a prism spreads them. Each color is a wavelength, red (long) to violet (short), with infrared and ultraviolet past the ends.
  • A thing's color is the light it bounces back after absorbing the rest. Oxygen-loaded hemoglobin is bright red and lets red light through.
  • The pulse ox compares red and infrared through the foot, using only the pulsing signal. Bili lights are blue because bilirubin absorbs blue and becomes flushable.
📈 Calculus 101 — reread
  • Calculus's second question: how much piles up? Total = rate × time. 3 mL/hr × 24 hr = 72 mL.
  • Rate as height, time as width: the total is the area under the line. Steps are rectangles added up.
  • When the rate is a curve, slice time into thin strips and add skinny rectangles; thinner slices get closer. ∫ is the symbol for that sum.
📊 Statistics 101 — reread
  • The median is the middle value after lining numbers up. One wild number drags the mean but not the median.
  • A percentile is your place in line out of 100. The 50th is the median. It's a position, not a grade.
  • Growth charts draw the 10th, 50th, 90th curves by age. Below the 10th = small for gestational age. Follow your own curve; crossing curves downward is the alarm.
🧬 Genetics 101 — reread
  • Gene → protein (enzyme) → job. A typo makes a broken enzyme, so what it should break down piles up. PKU: phenylalanine piles up — a special diet prevents the harm.
  • The heel prick (Guthrie, 1961): blood spots on a card at 1–2 days, tested for about 35 hidden, treatable conditions. Hearing and pulse-ox heart screens ride along.
  • Screen everyone when a disease hides, waiting harms, early treatment works, and the test is cheap. A positive screen means “test again, properly, now.”
🧠 Psychology 101 — reread
  • Attachment is the built-in bond that makes a baby seek a caregiver as a safe base — a survival system, not a side effect of being fed.
  • Harlow's monkeys chose comfort over food; Ainsworth found secure babies (about 6 in 10) have reliably responsive caregivers; Tronick's still face shows babies fight to restart serve-and-return.
  • A NICU strains the rope. Measured: early holding, kangaroo care (about a quarter fewer deaths), and parents doing the care all help. Bonding is treatment; parents are on the team.
🏥 Sociology 101 — reread
  • About 13 million babies a year are born preterm and about 900,000 die of it. In the poorest countries about half of very early babies die for lack of basic care.
  • Compare groups with rates (per 100, per 1,000), not counts. In the US, Black babies die in the first year at more than twice the rate of white babies.
  • The gaps come from social determinants — money, food, housing, stress, distance, being listened to — not from the babies. Change the conditions and the numbers move.
