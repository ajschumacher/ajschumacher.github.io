# The Four-Way Test


1. Is it the TRUTH?
2. Is it FAIR to all concerned?
3. Will it build GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS?
4. Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?


---

“The Four-Way Test of the things we think, say or do” was [written][]
in 1932 by Herbert J. Taylor as part of turning around his aluminum
company. It was adopted by Rotary International in the 1940s. I have
kept the capitalization that they seem to use.

[written]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Four-Way_Test


The Four-Way Test is very reminiscent of an 1872 [poem][] by Mary Ann
Pietzker, “Is it True? Is it Necessary? Is it Kind?” Hard to say
whether there's a connection.

[poem]: https://books.google.at/books?id=tXMCAAAAQAAJ&vq=before%20you%20propose%20to%20speak&hl=de&pg=PA54#v=onepage&q=before%20you%20propose%20to%20speak&f=false


I think the form of the test, as questions rather than prescriptions,
is an interesting alternative to a lot of the lists of virtues, and
laws that mostly say what not to do.
