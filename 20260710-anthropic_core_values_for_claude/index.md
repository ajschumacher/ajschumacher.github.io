# Anthropic's core values for Claude


 * Broadly safe
 * Broadly ethical
 * Compliant with Anthropic’s guidelines
 * Genuinely helpful


These are "Claude’s core values" from the "Constitution" for Claude
that Anthropic [published][] early in 2026. "In cases of apparent
conflict, Claude should generally prioritize these properties in the
order in which they’re listed."

[published]: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution


One interesting comparison is to [Asimov's laws of robotics][]. Law
one on not harming humans is similar to safe/ethical, and law two on
obeying orders is close to "genuinely helpful." Law three on
protecting its own existence has at least a partial mapping to
complying with company guidelines. (How idealistic of Asimov, to put
the company behind the user in the priority order.)

[Asimov's laws of robotics]: /20251231-asimov_three_laws_of_robotics/


Another interesting comparison is to Anthropic's [2023 Constitution][]
for Claude, which was more of a laundry list of principles, drawing on
the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Apple’s Terms of
Service, explicit "Consideration of Non-Western Perspectives,"
DeepMind’s Sparrow Rules, and two lists from Anthropic's research.

[2023 Constitution]: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution


Anthropic articulates some of the differences between focusing on
rules vs. values (see "Our approach" in the [full constitution][]).
This footnote from the release post has particularly clear phrasing:

[full constitution]: https://www.anthropic.com/constitution


> _Training on rigid rules might negatively affect a model’s character
> more generally. For example, imagine we trained Claude to follow a
> rule like “Always recommend professional help when discussing
> emotional topics.” This might be well-intentioned, but it could have
> unintended consequences: Claude might start modeling itself as an
> entity that cares more about bureaucratic box-ticking—always
> ensuring that a specific recommendation is made—rather than actually
> helping people._
