# “Mathematics is” quotes


For at least six years, I've been [collecting][] quotes about what
mathematics is. Here are some of them.

[collecting]: https://github.com/ajschumacher/ajschumacher.github.io/issues/205


---

> “... mathematics is not an empirical science, ...” (Von Neumann,
> [The Mathematician][])

[The Mathematician]: https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Von_Neumann_Part_1/


---

> “Mathematics is not a deductive science—that's a cliché.” (Halmos,
> [I want to be a mathematician][])

[I want to be a mathematician]: /20210821-i_want_to_be_a_mathematician_by_halmos/


---

> “Pure mathematics is, in its way, the poetry of logical ideas.”
> (Einstein, epitaph for Noether)


---

> “... mathematics is the science of skillful operations with concepts
> and rules invented just for this purpose.” (Wigner,
> [The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics in the Natural Sciences][])

[The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics in the Natural Sciences]: https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/wigner.pdf


---

> “Mathematics is a tool for reasoning.” (Feynman,
> [The Relation of Mathematics to Physics][])

[The Relation of Mathematics to Physics]: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IaSN-3JAVTg


---

> “Mathematics is no longer knowledge but a game of formulae, ruled by
> certain conventions, which is very well comparable to the game of
> chess.” (Weyl, Current Epistemological Situation in Mathematics)


---

> “Mathematics is the art of giving the same name to different
> things.” (Poincaré, [Science et Methode][])

[Science et Methode]: https://www.nieuwarchief.nl/serie5/pdf/naw5-2012-13-3-154.pdf


---

> “... mathematics cannot amount to anything more than an immense
> tautology.” (Poincaré, [Science and Hypothesis][])

[Science and Hypothesis]: /20230116-ten_equations_that_rule_the_world/


---

> “Mathematics is the extension of common sense by other means.”
> (Ellenberg, [How Not to Be Wrong][])


---

> “Mathematics is the art of explanation.” (Lockhart,
> [A Mathematician's Lament][])

[A Mathematician's Lament]: /20200909-mathematicians_lament_by_lockhart/


---

> “Mathematics is the study of things that come out a certain way
> because there is no other way they could possibly be.” (Ellenberg,
> [How Not to Be Wrong][])

[How Not to Be Wrong]: https://planspace.org/20200925-how_not_to_be_wrong_by_ellenberg/


---

> “[Mathematics is] about how things fit together.” (Kra,
> [Inside Dynamical Systems and the Mathematics of Change][])

[Inside Dynamical Systems and the Mathematics of Change]: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZaEVoH9Z4k


---

> “[Mathematics] is security. Certainty. Truth. Beauty. Insight.
> Structure. Architecture.” (Halmos, quoted in his MAA obituary)


---

> “Mathematics is prestidigitation.” (Linderholm, Mathematics Made
> Difficult)


---

> “Mathematics is the science of the infinite.” (Weyl)


---

> “Mathematics is the study of anything that obeys the rules of logic,
> using the rules of logic.” (Cheng, [How to bake π][])

[How to bake π]: /20260728-how_to_bake_pi_by_cheng/


---

> “Mathematics is easy, as long as you have the right definition of
> ‘easy’” (Cheng, [How to bake π][])


---

> “The pursuit of mathematics is the process of working out exactly
> what is easy, and the process of making as many things easy as
> possible.” (Cheng, [How to bake π][])


---

> “Mathematics is interesting in so far as it occupies our reasoning
> and inventive powers.” (Pólya, [How to Solve It][])

[How to Solve It]: /2013/09/28/polyas-how-to-solve-it-quotes-and-comments/


---

> “Mathematics is about problems, and problems must be made the focus
> of a student’s mathematical life.” (Lockhart,
> [A Mathematician's Lament][])


---

> “Mathematics is not life” (Cheng, [How to bake π][])


---

> “In the end mathematics is simply about things that are true.”
> (Cheng, [How to bake π][])


---

> “Mathematics is not settled.” (Ellenberg, [How Not to Be Wrong][])
