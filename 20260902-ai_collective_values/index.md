# AI Collective community and team values


 * Community
     * Connection
     * Positive Sum
     * Curiosity
     * Belonging
 * Team
     * Ambition
     * Accountability
     * Adaptability
     * Collectivism


[The AI Collective][] is a non-profit that hosts events. It doesn't
totally make sense to me, but they seem to enjoy doing what they do,
and the events can be fun.

[The AI Collective]: https://www.aicollective.com/


They have two lists of [values][], or maybe one list with two parts.
One is for the "community," which I think means anyone who comes to
their events. The other is for the "team," which seems to be the staff
and organizers—the people _running_ the AI Collective. It's an
interesting divide, somewhat like the divide between management and
non-management, or capital and labor.

[values]: https://www.aicollective.com/values
